Our Story

A small-town burger stand, the way it should be

Fatbelly Burgers is a little red shop on Main Street with a big appetite. We serve only Crystal River Meats: 100% grass-fed, Colorado-raised beef and lamb, cooked on a hot griddle and piled high on a toasted bun.

Grab a table on the flower-lined patio, bring the kids, bring the dog, and stay a while. Locals call us one of the least pretentious spots in town, and we take that as a compliment.
